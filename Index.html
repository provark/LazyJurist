// THE BENCH //
You are the judge. Choose a subject and decide up to ten motions.
  • RULESwipe left to DENY, right to GRANT. Keyboard: ← and →.
  • STUDYUntimed practice. Mistakes never end your docket early.
  • CHALLENGE45/55/65 seconds by difficulty. Every 2 overtime seconds reduces your award or adds 1 point to a loss. Correct answers cannot lose reputation.
  • STREAK3 correct in a row earns JUDICIAL AUTHORITY — base reputation loss is halved in Challenge mode.
  • WARNING3 wrong in a row triggers a BAR ASSOC. WARNING. One more wrong removes you in Challenge mode. Any correct answer breaks the wrong streak.
  • SPLITSome motions are genuinely contested. Either ruling is defensible; splits are tracked separately from accuracy and do not change your streak.
  • REPChallenge reputation starts at 50. Hit 0 and you're off the bench. Study mode always lets you continue.
